## What shapes buying in Baden-Württemberg

The state raised the rate from 3.5 to 5% in November 2011 and has not changed it since. Relief for first-time buyers has been debated, but as of 2026 nothing has been passed: the full rate applies to every purchase.

Baden-Württemberg is a typical “split” state for broker fees: 7.14% including VAT is customary, shared equally by buyer and seller. Many engineers and IT specialists moving to the Stuttgart region are surprised how quickly closing costs reach five figures; buying without a broker saves a noticeable share of your capital.
