## What shapes buying in Bavaria

Since 2006 the states set the transfer tax rate themselves, and all of them raised it – except Bavaria. The state government uses the low rate as a selling point. For buyers with little capital it is a real lever: closing costs without a broker are about 5.5% here instead of up to 8.5% elsewhere.

The catch is the price level: Munich and its surroundings, but also Regensburg and Ingolstadt, have some of the highest prices per square metre in the country. For broker fees Bavaria is a split state with 7.14% customary, half paid by the buyer.
