## What shapes buying in Berlin

In 2007 Berlin was the first state to raise the rate above 3.5%; since 2014 it has been 6%. As most purchases are flats in apartment buildings, you will often also want a reserve for the owners' association – not part of the closing costs, but worth planning into your capital.

The customary broker fee in Berlin is 7.14% including VAT, of which the buyer pays half. If you know the “orderer principle” from renting: it does not apply to purchases. Here the split rule of § 656c BGB applies instead.
