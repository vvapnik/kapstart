## What shapes buying in Brandenburg

Brandenburg raised the rate to 5% in 2011 and to 6.5% in 2015, joining the top group with North Rhine-Westphalia, Saarland and Schleswig-Holstein. In the Berlin commuter belt – Potsdam, Falkensee, Bernau, Königs Wusterhausen – this is noticeable, because prices there are close to Berlin's.

The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally. In rural areas further from Berlin houses are much cheaper and private sales without a broker are more common.
