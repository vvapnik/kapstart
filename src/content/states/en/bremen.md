## What shapes buying in Bremen

The state of Bremen consists of two cities, Bremen and Bremerhaven, with very different price levels: in Bremerhaven a home often costs half of what it does in popular Bremen districts such as Schwachhausen or Findorff.

The customary broker fee in Bremen is 5.95% including VAT, below the 7.14% common in much of Germany. The buyer pays half, just under 3%, which partly offsets the 2025 tax increase.
