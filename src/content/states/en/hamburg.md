## What shapes buying in Hamburg

Hamburg charged 4.5% for many years and moved to 5.5% on 1 January 2023. The city is mostly a market for flats; detached houses are found mainly in the Walddörfer, in Bergedorf and south of the Elbe.

Hamburg's customary broker fee is 6.25% including VAT in total, half paid by the buyer. Many buyers also look in the surroundings – but note: Norderstedt, Ahrensburg and Pinneberg are in Schleswig-Holstein with 6.5% transfer tax, Buxtehude and Seevetal in Lower Saxony with 5%.
