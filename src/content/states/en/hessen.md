## What shapes buying in Hesse

Hesse raised the rate to 5% in 2013 and to 6% in August 2014. Since March 2024 there is the Hessengeld: first-time buyers of an owner-occupied home receive €10,000 per buyer plus €5,000 per child, at most the transfer tax paid, spread over ten years. Kapstart does not count it: the tax is still due right after the purchase and must be paid from your capital.

The customary broker fee in Hesse is 5.95% including VAT, so the buyer pays just under 3%. The Rhine-Main area with Frankfurt, Offenbach and Wiesbaden – home to many international finance and IT jobs – is expensive; northern and central Hesse are much cheaper.
