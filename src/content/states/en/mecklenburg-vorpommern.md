## What shapes buying in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania

The state moved to 5% in 2012 and to 6% in July 2019. At the same time it introduced a programme for families with children buying their first home: a grant of up to €10,000 towards the transfer tax. It is applied for after the purchase, so your capital still has to cover the tax first.

The market is split: Rostock, Greifswald and the Baltic coast are expensive, the interior often very cheap. 5.95% broker fee including VAT is customary, split equally.
