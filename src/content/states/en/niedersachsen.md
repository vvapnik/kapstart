## What shapes buying in Lower Saxony

Lower Saxony raised the rate to 4.5% in 2011 and to 5% in 2014. The state is large and varied: Hanover, Brunswick, Oldenburg and the Hamburg commuter belt are in demand; much of the south and east is considerably cheaper.

There is no single broker fee level. In some regions 7.14% in total is customary, in others – such as the Hanover area and parts of the north-west – rather 4.76 to 5.95%. Kapstart uses the higher value so that your capital is enough in any case. In the calculator you can enter your actual buyer's share under “Assumptions”.
