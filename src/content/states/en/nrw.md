## What shapes buying in NRW

NRW raised the rate to 5% in October 2011 and to 6.5% in January 2015. An allowance for first-time buyers has been discussed for years but, as of 2026, has not been introduced. Instead, the state bank NRW.BANK supports purchases with loans.

Germany's most populous state has very different markets: Cologne, Düsseldorf and Münster are expensive, the Ruhr area and parts of East Westphalia and the Sauerland much cheaper. The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally. Because of the high tax rate, the same capital buys a house about a third cheaper here than in Bavaria.
