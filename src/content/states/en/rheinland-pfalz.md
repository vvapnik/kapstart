## What shapes buying in Rhineland-Palatinate

The 5% rate has applied since 1 March 2012, noticeably less than in neighbouring Saarland (6.5%) and Hesse (6%). For commuters to Frankfurt, Wiesbaden or Luxembourg, searching on the Rhineland-Palatinate side can pay off.

Mainz, the Frankfurt commuter belt and the Trier region (close to Luxembourg) are expensive; the Eifel, the Hunsrück and the Western Palatinate are much cheaper. 7.14% broker fee including VAT is customary, split equally.
