## What shapes buying in Saarland

Saarland moved to 4% in 2011, 4.5% in 2012, 5.5% in 2013 and 6.5% in 2015. In percentage terms its closing costs are as high as in NRW, Brandenburg and Schleswig-Holstein.

In euros, buying is often still cheap, because houses in Saarland are among the most affordable in western Germany. Cross-border commuters to France and Luxembourg buy here frequently. The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally.
