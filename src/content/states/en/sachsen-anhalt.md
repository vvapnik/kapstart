## What shapes buying in Saxony-Anhalt

Saxony-Anhalt raised the rate to 4.5% in March 2010 and to 5% in March 2012; it has not changed since. Halle and Magdeburg are the most sought-after markets, and new industrial investment has pushed up demand around Magdeburg in recent years.

Outside the cities, houses are often available for less than €200,000. The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally; private sales without a broker are common in the countryside.
