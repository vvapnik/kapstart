## What shapes buying in Saxony

The jump from 3.5 to 5.5% on 1 January 2023 was large: closing costs without a broker rose from about 5.5 to 7.5% of the price. Anyone who signed the contract just before the turn of the year still paid the old rate – what counts is the date of the purchase contract.

Leipzig and Dresden are in demand and much more expensive than Chemnitz, the Vogtland or Lusatia. The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally.
