## What shapes buying in Schleswig-Holstein

The state raised the rate to 5% in 2012 and to 6.5% in 2014. Many buyers come from Hamburg to the surrounding towns – Norderstedt, Ahrensburg, Pinneberg or Bad Segeberg – and pay a percentage point more tax there than in the city.

Kiel, Lübeck and the North Sea and Baltic resorts are expensive, the interior cheaper. The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally; around Hamburg, Hamburg's 6.25% is sometimes used.
