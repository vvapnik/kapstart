## What shapes buying in Thuringia

Thuringia moved to 5% in 2011 and to 6.5% in 2017. On 1 January 2024 the state parliament cut the rate back to 5% – a rare case. For buyers this means needing about 1.5% of the price less capital than until the end of 2023.

Erfurt, Jena and Weimar are the most sought-after cities; Jena, with its university and optics industry, is relatively expensive. Prices are low in the countryside and in eastern Thuringia. The customary broker fee is 7.14% including VAT, split equally.
